## What is NOT proven — do not claim it

- Any conversion, productivity or efficiency percentage. No before/after figures are
  documented.
- Any NPS, satisfaction or completion statistic.
- Any claim of being largest, fastest or cheapest in the market.
- Named client outcomes where permission to name has not been confirmed — anonymise to
  "a leading private sector bank" or "a South India based NBFC".
