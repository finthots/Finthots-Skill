# Who to target inside a lending company

FinThots sells training. Training is bought by a committee, not a person. Build
every company's contact set across these buckets.

## Bucket 1 — L&D (the budget holder)

The annual training budget and the eLearning library spend sit here. Primary
target for every segment.

Titles to search:
- Chief Learning Officer
- Head / National Head – Learning & Development
- Head – Training, Head – Capability Building
- VP / AVP / Senior Manager – L&D
- National Training Manager, Zonal / Regional Training Manager
- Head – Digital Learning, LMS Manager, LMS Administrator
- Head – Staff Training College / Training Academy (bank-linked lenders:
  LIC HFL, PNB HF, Can Fin, Repco, PSU banks, co-operative banks)
- Head – Talent Management / OD, where no standalone L&D function exists
- Instructional Designer, Learning Experience Designer (useful as an
  influencer and a warm referral into the head)

Note: regional and zonal training managers have no budget but are excellent
intelligence and referral sources. Keep them, mark `Seniority = Manager`,
and do not spend mobile credits on them.

## Bucket 2 — Credit / Business (the pain owner)

They do not hold the budget but they create the demand that forces L&D to
spend. Usually the faster-converting entry point for functional content.

Titles to search:
- Chief Credit Officer, National Credit Manager, Head – Credit
- Head – Credit Policy, Head – Credit Underwriting
- Chief Risk Officer, Head – Credit Risk
- Chief Business Officer, National Sales Manager, Head – Retail Business
- Business Head – Affordable / Prime / Mortgages / LAP
- Head – Collections
- Head – Technical & Legal, Head – Valuations (property visit content)
- Head – Operations, Head – Process Excellence (process simulations,
  policy decoding)

## Bucket 3 — Compliance (the easiest door)

Budget is annual, regulator-driven and non-discretionary. The conversation is
"who delivers it this year", not "do we need it". Lower ticket, recurring,
cheapest route into a new logo.

Titles to search:
- Chief Compliance Officer
- Principal Officer – AML / KYC (a statutory named role; easy to find)
- Head – Internal Audit
- Head – Fraud Risk Management, Head – Vigilance
- Presiding Officer – Internal Committee (POSH; usually senior HR or Legal)

## Bucket 4 — HR / Gate

Sponsor at small companies, a cc at large ones. Also the route to induction and
mass hire-and-train work.

Titles to search:
- CHRO, Chief People Officer, Head – Human Resources
- Head – Talent Acquisition (owns induction and mass hire-train)
- Head – Procurement / Vendor Management

**Procurement warning:** most large lenders require vendor empanelment before
any PO can be raised, and that takes 4-8 weeks on its own. Start empanelment in
parallel with Tier 1 outreach, never after a verbal yes.

## Bucket 5 — Executive

- MD & CEO, Whole-Time Director, Chief Business Officer

**Only worth targeting at Tier 3.** At a small or affordable lender the MD
genuinely signs off a ₹10-15 lakh training spend and is reachable. At a large
lender, writing to the MD gets no reply and signals that you do not know the
industry. Skip.

## Who to skip entirely

- Branch managers, area managers, individual sales staff — no budget, no
  upward influence
- Chief Technology / Digital / Marketing officers, unless the pitch is
  specifically app simulation or customer-facing content
- Anyone whose title contains only "Executive", "Associate" or "Trainee"

## Tiering rule

| Tier | Definition | Contacts per company | Personas |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Large: AUM > ₹25,000 cr or headcount > 5,000 | 6-8 | L&D + digital learning + credit/business + risk + compliance + TA + procurement |
| 2 | Mid: AUM ₹5,000-25,000 cr | 3-4 | L&D or HR head + credit head + compliance officer |
| 3 | Small / affordable: AUM < ₹5,000 cr | 2-3 | MD or CBO + HR head + credit or business head |

If AUM is unavailable, tier on employee count or on whether the company has a
dedicated L&D function at all — the absence of one is itself a Tier 3 signal.

## Persona mix sanity check

Before enriching, count the list by persona. If L&D is under 25% of rows, or
Credit/Business and Compliance are near zero, the list came off company
"leadership team" pages, which only list CXOs. Go back to LinkedIn or a people
search tool and fill the layer below before spending any credits.
